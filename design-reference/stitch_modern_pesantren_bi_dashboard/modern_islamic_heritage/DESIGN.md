## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the dignified lineage and forward-looking spirit of Pondok Pesantren Damanhuri Romly (Genggong Probolinggo). It combines the sacred sobriety of classical Islamic scholarship (*pesantren salaf*) with the crisp efficiency of modern executive enterprise tools. 

### Brand Personality & Core Attributes
- **Waqār (Dignified Gravity):** Grounded, tranquil, and authoritative without feeling austere or archaic.
- **Barakah & Warmth:** Organic warmth derived from sunlit stone, parchment, and aged sand rather than clinical modern whites.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Governed by rhythm, clean geometric proportions, subtle micro-borders, and high-legibility metrics.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Serving santri administrators, scholarly councils (*majlis kyai*), donors, and guardians. The UI evokes tranquility, high-trust transparency, scholarly prestige, and effortless administrative clarity.

### Visual Philosophy: Modern Pesantren Aesthetic 2026
The aesthetic blends contemporary editorial minimalism with curated Islamic architectural cues:
- **Tonal Hierarchy:** Deep forest greens and warm ivories replace harsh pure black-and-white motifs.
- **Heritage Accents:** Antique gold serves purely for prestige markers, ceremonial iconography, active indicators, and high-value status flags.
- **Functional Structure:** Crisp surfaces anchored by subtle warm sand micro-borders, generous breathing space, and clear tabular layout systems.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from classical Islamic architecture, manuscript illumination, and natural sanctuary materials:

- **Primary (`#133E32` - Deep Pine / Islamic Forest Green):** The institutional core. Used for brand anchors, primary action buttons, key header surfaces, and high-priority navigation.
- **Secondary (`#C5A15B` - Antique Gold / Warm Ochre):** The emblem of scholarly heritage. Reserved for badges, selected states, highlights, visual emblems, and decorative dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#A95135` - Terracotta / Clay):** The accent tone for urgent operational alerts, deadline indicators, fee arrears notices, and secondary emphasis tags.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF9F4` / `#F5F2EB` - Warm Ivory / Desert Sand):** The foundation canvas. Replaces standard stark cool whites with a restful, parchment-like natural ground.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for interactive cards, data panels, and elevated functional containers.
- **Typography Base (`#1E2522` - Deep Charcoal Slate):** A tinted slate black carrying a whisper of green, ensuring comfortable prolonged reading.
- **Structural Lines (`#E2DBD0` - Soft Sand Border):** Used for micro-borders, card divisions, and tabular separation lines.

## Typography

The typographic system harmonizes the dignified editorial authority of classical Islamic scholarship with modern administrative UI requirements:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for prestigious titles, major dashboard welcomes, formal institutional notices, and certificate headings. Uses generous line heights and balanced letter-spacing to prevent visual crowding.
- **Operational Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers all system metrics, interactive forms, navigation links, and administrative data sets. Its modern geometric profile ensures instant legibility across complex schedules, records, and tables.
- **Bilingual & Arabic Support:** When rendering classical kitab texts, Quranic verses, or Arabic honorifics, paired system font fallbacks (`Amiri`, `Scheherazade New`, or `Noto Naskh Arabic`) must match the visual baseline with 125% line-height scaling compared to Latin script equivalents.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-tier institutional information with executive composure.

### Layout Rules & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns. Gutter is `1rem`, canvas margin is `1rem`. Floating bottom action navigation replaces complex sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns. Gutter is `1.25rem`, canvas margin is `1.5rem`. Side navigation condenses into an icon-rail.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns. Gutter is `1.5rem`, canvas margin is `2.5rem`. Fixed dual-level navigation (Executive Masthead + Contextual Side Rail). Max content container: `1440px`.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm is deliberate and spacious, mirroring Islamic architecture's courtyards (*sahn*). Dense administrative controls are nested inside generous white card surfaces, preventing information overload during daily academic management.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud, synthetic drop shadows. Hierarchy is achieved through **Tonal Surface Stacking** reinforced by **Warm Sand Micro-Borders**.

- **Canvas Ground (Level 0):** `#FBF9F4` base wash. Completely flat, evoking vellum paper.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` pure white fill surrounded by a `1px` continuous micro-border of `#E2DBD0`. Elevation is anchored with an ultra-soft, warm ambient glow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(19, 62, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals, Dropdowns & Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` fill with an elevated warm border (`#D6CEBE`) and dual shadow layering: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(19, 62, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(19, 62, 50, 0.03)`.
- **Active / Focused Depth:** Input fields, table row hovers, and active cards exchange their sand border for an antique gold accent ring (`#C5A15B`) at `1.5px` stroke with no additional harsh shadow.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses the **Soft (Level 1)** setting:
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Checkboxes, status tags, inline code badges, and small utility buttons.
- **Cards & Primary Blocks (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Main content cards, form containers, data tables, and modal dialogs.
- **Executive Elements (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / `12px`):** Standalone hero banner units and featured callout modules.

This controlled corner radius retains the architectural precision and solemnity required for a venerable Islamic institution, avoiding overly playful or child-like pill forms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Pine background (`#133E32`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` border radius, subtle hover transition shift to a darker green (`#0C2921`).
- **Secondary (Heritage):** Soft Ivory fill (`#F5F2EB`) with Antique Gold micro-border (`#C5A15B`) and deep pine text.
- **Tertiary / Warning:** Terracotta background (`#A95135`) with white text, strictly for deletion, disciplinary logs, or unpaid tuition warnings.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Pesantren Heritage Badge:** `#C5A15B` background at 15% opacity with solid Antique Gold text (`#9A7A38`) and a 1px border.
- **Active / Verified Tag:** Light emerald tint (`#E8F2EE`) with Deep Pine text (`#133E32`).
- **Notice / Arrears Tag:** Clay tint (`#F9ECE8`) with Terracotta text (`#A95135`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input fields use `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with an `#E2DBD0` border.
- Placeholders are muted charcoal slate (`#1E2522` at 40% opacity).
- Focused states feature a crisp `1.5px` border in `#C5A15B` with zero outer neon glow.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons use Deep Pine fill when active, with gold checkmark glyphs.

### Cards & Data Panels
- Pure white surfaces set against `#FBF9F4` with a crisp `1px solid #E2DBD0` boundary.
- Card headers utilize Playfair Display (`headline-sm` or `title-md`) accompanied by a subtle 2px Antique Gold hairline accent divider under the section title.

### Specialized Pesantren Components
- **Kitab & Halaqah Roster Row:** Dual-script list layout with Latin student credentials left-aligned and Arabic course/sanad titles right-aligned in classical Naskh typography.
- **Hijri-Masehi Dual Calendar Tile:** Compact split-date display featuring Gregorian figures in Plus Jakarta Sans above gold-tinted Hijri references.
